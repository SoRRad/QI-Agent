**This document is a placeholder and must be replaced with institutional
policy before the Ask feature can answer any question about what a project
needs in order to be approved here.** The intake wizard's own validation
enforces the methodological requirements from the build standard — a
five-element aim, a balancing measure, a named clinical owner — but it cannot
enforce institutional requirements it has not been told about.

The institution must supply the following.

1. **Required sign-offs** and their order. A trainee needs to know whose
   signature is needed before starting, not after.
2. **Faculty sponsorship** — required or not, and who is eligible.
3. **The minimum data-access arrangement** that must exist before approval.
   Projects that are approved without a data route are the ones that stall.
4. **Cross-program and cross-department rules**, including who arbitrates.
5. **EHR-touching projects** — what is permitted, and the approval route for
   configuration changes, order sets, or alerts.
6. **Patient-facing materials** — whether trainee projects may produce them,
   and the review route if so.
7. **Review cadence and cut-off dates**, so intake can tell a trainee when
   their submission will actually be seen.
8. **Grounds for declining or deferring**, stated plainly. A committee that
   declines projects for unstated reasons trains people not to submit.

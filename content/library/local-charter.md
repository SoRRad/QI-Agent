**This document is a placeholder and must be replaced with the institution's
adopted charter before the Ask feature can answer any question about the
committee's authority, membership, or escalation route.**

The charter is the document that determines what this system can legitimately
claim. The barriers log escalates themes from `raised` through `at GMEC` to
`decided` and `closed`; that lifecycle is only meaningful if the escalation
route it models is the real one.

The institution must supply the following.

1. **Formal name and reporting line** — which body this committee reports to.
2. **Scope of authority.** What may this committee decide, and what may it only
   recommend? Trainees will read a `decided` barrier as a commitment.
3. **Voting membership**, including how many trainee seats exist and how they
   are filled.
4. **Quorum and decision rules.**
5. **Meeting frequency and the annual calendar**, which the event kits and the
   pulse survey cadence are built around.
6. **The escalation route to GMEC**, and which matters must be escalated rather
   than decided locally.
7. **The chair's term and appointment.**
8. **Relationship to patient safety and quality departments**, including who
   owns what. This boundary matters: this system is not an approved route for
   safety event reporting, and the charter should say where that goes instead.
9. **What the committee is explicitly not responsible for.** The absence of this
   section is what causes a QI committee to accumulate everything nobody else
   wants.

**This document is a placeholder and must be replaced with institutional
policy before the Ask feature can answer any question about IRB or QI
determination.** Until it is replaced, the system will tell users that this
topic is not covered rather than answer from general knowledge, and any answer
citing this document carries a banner saying so.

The institution must supply the following. Each item is something a trainee
will be asked by a reviewer, an editor, or a program director, and none of it
can be inferred from published guidance — determination practice is local.

1. **The determining office.** Name, contact, and who to ask when unsure.
2. **The written criteria** this institution uses to distinguish quality
   improvement from human subjects research. General frameworks exist; what
   matters here is the local reading of them.
3. **The request form or portal**, and the route for submitting it.
4. **Turnaround time**, so a trainee can plan around it.
5. **When a determination is required** — before data collection, or only
   before dissemination. This is the single most consequential item on the
   list, because getting it wrong retroactively can make completed work
   unpublishable.
6. **Policy on undetermined work** that someone now wishes to present or
   publish.
7. **Policy on data leaving the institution**, including de-identified data,
   and including conference slides.
8. **The named signatory** who may sign a determination memo.

Until this document is replaced, the IRB pre-check questionnaire will produce
a draft memo marked as requiring local confirmation, and will flag rather than
resolve any genuinely ambiguous answer.
